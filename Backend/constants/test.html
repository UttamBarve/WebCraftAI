Our Story

A Legacy of Culinary Excellence

Founded in 2018 by Chef Marcus Aurelius, Aurum was born from a vision to elevate dining into an art form. With over two decades of experience in Michelin-starred kitchens across Europe, Chef Marcus brings a philosophy rooted in respect for ingredients, technique, and the guest experience.

Every dish tells a story — of the farmer who grew the vegetables, the fisherman who caught the seafood, the artisan who aged the cheese. We honor these stories by letting ingredients shine through thoughtful preparation and artistic presentation.
